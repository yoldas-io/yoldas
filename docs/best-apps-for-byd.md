## Navigation: Google Maps and Waze

Google Maps and Waze are the two most used apps for traffic and routes. Google Maps is built into new BYDs with Google; on the others it is installed as an APK. With Yoldaş you bring the map to the cluster behind the steering wheel: [map on the cluster](https://yoldas.io/en/cluster-display).

## Music: Spotify

In some markets Spotify comes built into the BYD screen and you sign in with a QR code. When Yoldaş splits the screen, Spotify stays open next to the map; the song that is playing also shows on the music card on the bottom bar.

## Video: YouTube

YouTube came built in on many models with BYD's 2025 update. It is ideal for watching while you wait for charging; with Yoldaş, YouTube is in one pane and your car's status is next to it. Details are in the [YouTube, Netflix, Google Maps on a BYD](https://yoldas.io/en/guides/byd-youtube-netflix-google-maps) guide.

## Phone: CarPlay and Android Auto

The easiest way to use your phone's map and music. It works wirelessly on most BYDs. For connecting and for CarPlay on the cluster, see the [BYD CarPlay and Android Auto](https://yoldas.io/en/guides/byd-carplay-android-auto) guide.

## Safety

Use video apps only while the car is parked or charging; watching video while driving is dangerous and illegal in many countries.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which apps can be installed on a BYD?

The DiLink screen is Android-based; apps such as Google Maps, Waze, Spotify and YouTube, as well as Yoldaş, can be installed as an APK. Some apps come built into a BYD. The ways to install them are in the APK installation guide.

### Does BYD have Google Play?

On new models with Google built in, such as the Atto 2 DM-i and Atto 3 EVO, Google Play and Google Maps are built in. Other models do not have Google Play; some cars have BYD's own app store (BYD Store).

### What is the best dashcam app for BYD?

On most models, BYD's own cameras do not record. Yoldaş records with the car's four cameras while driving and parked, and sends a Sentry notification while parked; no extra camera is needed.

### Can you watch YouTube on a BYD?

Yes, on many models YouTube came built in with an update. Use video apps only while parked or charging.
