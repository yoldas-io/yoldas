## Split the screen: map, music and video side by side

BYD's centre screen is big, but it is given over to a single app. Yoldaş splits it into two or three panes; in each pane you put an app, CarPlay, Android Auto, the Yoldaş home screen or the On the road scene. You drag the divider between the panes to set the ratio.

- **While driving:** Google Maps or Waze on one side, and on the other Spotify or the On the road scene, which shows your car on the road.
- **While charging:** YouTube or Netflix on one side, and on the other the Yoldaş home screen from your charging layout: charging power and charge level.
- **While parked:** a video on one side, the Yoldaş home screen and your 3D car on the other.

You set up separate layouts for parking, driving and charging; they change on their own with the gear and the charging cable.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does BYD have YouTube?

Yes. With its July 2025 update, BYD brought YouTube built in to models such as the Seal U, Atto 3 and Dolphin in Turkey; the same update was released in Brazil. Watch YouTube only while the car is parked or charging.

### Can I watch Netflix on a BYD?

Netflix is not in BYD's app store. When it is installed separately, some cars are reported to show a "device not supported" warning. Use video apps only while parked or charging.

### Can I use Google Maps or Waze on a BYD?

On new models with Google built in, such as the Atto 2 DM-i and Atto 3 EVO, Google Maps is built in. On other models, Google Maps and Waze are installed separately. With Yoldaş you can also bring the map app you choose to the instrument cluster behind the steering wheel.

### Does BYD have Spotify?

In some markets Spotify comes built into the BYD screen and you sign in with a QR code. If not, it can be installed separately or played from your phone with CarPlay or Android Auto.

### Can I use two apps at the same time?

Yoldaş splits the screen into two or three panes: Google Maps on one side, Spotify on the other; while charging, YouTube on one side and the Yoldaş home screen on the other.
