# BYD 12 V battery dead: signs, fixes and monitoring

Even if your electric BYD's big battery is full, the car wakes up with the small 12 V battery. If the 12 V battery dies, the car won't open. This guide covers the signs, the fixes in BYD's manuals and how to spot a weak battery in advance: Yoldaş shows the 12 V voltage on the screen and on your phone at any time.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

## Signs

- The smart key can't find the car, and the doors won't open.
- The car won't start, and the screens don't turn on.
- The low-voltage system fault light comes on in the cluster.
- While charging, a "low-voltage battery over-discharged" error may appear.

## What to do if the car won't open

1. **Bring the key close to the door**

Hold the smart key near the driver's door and press the button on the door handle. On the Seal, if the power was cut after smart charging, press the button a few times, open the car and leave it switched on for at least an hour.
2. **Mechanical key**

If that doesn't work, open the door with the mechanical key.
3. **Jump-starting**

Use only the positive terminal in the fuse box under the bonnet and the negative point BYD shows. BYD recommends doing this with professional help. Don't use a BYD to jump-start another car.
4. **Service**

If the battery dies often, have a BYD service centre check the battery and its charging.

## Does BYD charge the 12 V battery on its own?

Yes, under certain conditions. According to BYD's manuals, when the car is off, the 12 V battery is low and drawing power from the big battery is allowed, "smart charging" starts by itself; it is normal to hear the car as if it were running. On the Seal, the bonnet must also be closed. Smart charging does not work when the big battery is very low; BYD recommends not leaving the car parked with a low charge for a long time. In cold weather the battery's capacity drops; have it checked before winter.

The battery type depends on the model: early Atto 3s are reported to use a lead-acid battery; the Seal and Sealion 7 have a lithium (LFP) 12 V battery with its own management system.

## Frequently asked questions

### What happens if the 12 V battery dies on a BYD?

The car does not recognise the key, the doors won't open and the car won't start; the low-voltage system fault light may come on in the cluster. Even if the big battery is full, the car wakes up with the 12 V battery.

### Does BYD charge the 12 V battery on its own?

Yes. According to BYD's manuals, when the car is off, the 12 V battery is low and the big battery has enough charge, "smart charging" starts by itself. It does not work when the big battery is very low; don't leave the car parked with a low charge for a long time.

### Can a BYD be jump-started?

Only from the jump-start points BYD shows, and preferably with professional help. Don't use a BYD to jump-start another car.

### How do I see the 12 V battery voltage?

Yoldaş shows the 12 V battery voltage on the home screen, on your phone and in the battery report. With the ready-made "12 V battery low" automation, you get a notification when the voltage drops.
