# Does BYD have sentry mode?

**Short answer: BYDs sold in Turkey and Europe do not have a factory sentry mode.** For now, BYD has added this feature by update only to some models in China. But your BYD already has four cameras: Yoldaş uses them to watch your parked car and alerts your phone when someone approaches.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

## Where does BYD's own sentry mode exist?

- **China:** since 2025, some models (for example the Yuan Plus, which is the Atto 3 in China, and the new Han) have received a "sentry mode" through a software update. While the car is locked, the cameras watch the surroundings and record if someone approaches or there is an impact. With a 2026 update, risky recordings are uploaded to the BYD app. BYD recommends a separate memory card for this, and it is reported to trigger often on busy streets.
- **Turkey and Europe:** BYD's official manuals and update notes have no sentry mode. BYD owners also report that BYD Turkey will not offer this feature.
- **Australia and Southeast Asia:** the manuals we checked (Atto 3, Atto 2, Dolphin, Sealion 5, 6 and 7) have no sentry mode.

## Sentry mode for your BYD with Yoldaş

Yoldaş is an app you install on your BYD's multimedia screen, and it uses the car's own cameras. No extra camera, SD card or cable is needed.

- **Phone notification:** while the car is parked, when an image that may show a person appears on the camera you choose, a notification reaches your phone right away; it says which camera it came from.
- **People, animals, vehicles:** images are checked in the car; changes such as headlight glare and raindrops are filtered out.
- **Radar and recording:** on your phone you see the direction of events on a radar. If parked recording is on, the video of the moment stays in Recordings.
- **Privacy:** images never leave the car; there is no face recognition.
- **Energy limit:** you set a lower limit for the battery and the 12 V battery; below that limit, Sentry stops.

## How to turn it on

1. **Install Yoldaş**

Get the APK from the [download page](https://yoldas.io/en/download) and install it as in the [installation guide](https://yoldas.io/en/install).
2. **Turn on Sentry**

On the Security tab in Yoldaş, choose the camera, download the recognition model once and turn on the "Sentry" switch. Sentry starts watching when the car is switched off.
3. **Pair your phone**

Open app.yoldas.io on your phone, scan the QR code on the car screen and turn on parked monitoring under Account › Notifications.

Sentry can be wrong and may not see every event; do not rely on it as a security measure on its own. Details are on the [Sentry Mode page](https://yoldas.io/en/sentry-mode). ## Frequently asked questions

### Does BYD have a sentry mode from the factory?

BYDs sold in Turkey and Europe do not have a factory sentry mode. In China it has been added to some models by software update since 2025. Yoldaş offers a sentry mode while parked, using BYD's own cameras.

### Do sentry alerts arrive in the BYD app?

Not in Turkey and Europe. With the updates in China, sentry recordings are uploaded to the BYD app. With Yoldaş, a notification reaches your phone when a person is seen while the car is parked.

### Do I need to buy an extra camera for sentry mode?

No. Yoldaş uses the car's own 360° cameras; no extra camera or SD card is needed.

### Which BYD models does Yoldaş's Sentry work on?

BYDs with DiLink: Seal, Seal U, Seal U DM-i, Sealion 7, Atto 3, Atto 2, Dolphin, Dolphin Surf, Han, Tang, Seal 6 DM-i and Sealion 5 DM-i.

### Does Sentry drain the battery?

Sentry runs while the car is off and uses energy. In Yoldaş you can set a lower limit for the battery and the 12 V battery; when a value drops below the limit, the connection stops by itself.
