## Frequently asked questions

### What should the BYD Atto 3 tyre pressure be?

According to BYD's Atto 3 manual, 2.5 bar (250 kPa, 36 psi) in all four tyres, when cold. On the Atto 3 EVO it is 2.7 bar. The exact value is on the label on your car's driver's door.

### What is the BYD Seal U tyre pressure?

For the Seal U DM-i, BYD's manual gives 2.5 bar at the front and 2.9 bar at the rear (cold). For the Seal U EV, a third-party source gives 2.5 bar. Check the door label on your car.

### Should I measure tyre pressure when the tyres are warm?

No. The values are for cold tyres. According to BYD manuals, a warm tyre after driving reads 0.3–0.4 bar higher; do not let air out while the tyres are warm.

### Where does tyre pressure show on the BYD screen?

On a BYD it shows on the instrument cluster and the vehicle information screen. With Yoldaş you see all four tyres together in the tyre widget on the home screen and on your phone.
