# BYD 360° camera: how to open it and watch it from your phone

Your BYD's four cameras show a bird's-eye view around the car while parking and manoeuvring. This guide covers the ways to open the camera and the transparent chassis view. The cameras themselves don't record and can't be watched from a phone; with Yoldaş, you can do both.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

## How to open the 360° camera

- **On the screen:** tap the car view button on the home screen.
- **On the steering wheel:** press the camera button.
- **In reverse:** it opens on its own when you shift into reverse.

On a landscape screen you tap the front, rear, left or right view; double-tapping the front or rear view opens a 180° wide view. On a portrait screen two views appear together; the radar icon adds obstacle warnings.

## Transparent chassis view

Tap the car image and the underside of the car turns transparent; you see kerbs and potholes under the wheels. When the car is switched on, the image from the last switch-off appears, and it refreshes once the car has moved forward its own length. When you drive slowly or stop often, the image can shift. Don't use it with the mirrors folded or the doors open.

## Care

The cameras are in the front bumper or grille, under the side mirrors and next to the rear number plate. Don't point a pressure washer directly at the cameras; wipe off water and dust promptly. If the screen says "no video signal detected", the camera may have opened before the multimedia system had fully started; wait a few seconds.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I open the 360-degree camera on a BYD?

Tap the car view button on the home screen, press the camera button on the steering wheel or shift into reverse. On a landscape screen you choose the front, rear, left or right view.

### Does BYD's 360 camera record?

No. According to BYD's privacy page, the panoramic camera only gives a live image and does not record. Yoldaş records with the same four cameras while driving and while parked.

### What is the transparent chassis view?

It is the view that shows the area under the car as if the car were not there. The image refreshes once the car has moved forward its own length; it can shift when you drive slowly and stop often.

### Can I watch the 360 camera from my phone?

Not with BYD's app. With Yoldaş you watch all four cameras live on your phone at the same time; if you like, you can listen to the cabin and talk to the people in the car.
