# BYD screen rotation: landscape and portrait

**Short answer: on BYDs with a rotating screen, press the screen button on the left of the steering wheel.** If there is no button, the navigation bar on the screen or a voice command also works. For cars with no button on the steering wheel, Yoldaş's "Rotate screen" shortcut turns the screen with one tap.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

## BYD's own ways

- **Steering wheel button:** on the Atto 3, Dolphin and Seal, the "screen mode" button with a two-rectangle icon on the left side of the steering wheel switches the screen between landscape and portrait.
- **Customisable button:** on the Seal U DM-i, the custom button on the steering wheel rotates the screen by default; it can be assigned to another function in the settings.
- **On-screen button:** on the Sealion 7 and Atto 3 there is a button in the screen's navigation bar that rotates the screen.
- **Voice command:** according to BYD's DiLink support page, the screen can also be rotated by voice command.

BYD manuals recommend using the screen in landscape while driving. CarPlay and Android Auto only work with the screen in landscape.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I rotate the BYD screen?

On BYDs with a rotating screen, press the screen button on the left side of the steering wheel (the two-rectangle icon). On some models the button in the screen's navigation bar or a voice command also rotates the screen.

### Does every BYD screen rotate?

No. On models such as the Atto 3, Dolphin, Seal, Seal U and Sealion 7 the screen rotates. With the new Atto 2, BYD began moving away from the rotating screen; on the Atto 3 EVO and Sealion 5 the screen is fixed in landscape.

### Do CarPlay and Android Auto work with the screen in portrait?

On a BYD, CarPlay and Android Auto only work with the screen in landscape; if the screen is in portrait, turn it to landscape.

### What can I do if there is no screen button on the steering wheel?

You can rotate the screen with one tap using the "Rotate screen" shortcut you add to the Yoldaş bottom bar or home screen. On cars whose screen does not rotate, this button appears greyed out.
