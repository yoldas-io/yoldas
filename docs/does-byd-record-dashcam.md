# Do BYD cameras record?

**Short answer: BYD's 360° cameras do not record on their own.** In Turkey and Europe these cameras only show a live image while parking. Some models have a separate recorder that only faces forward. Yoldaş records with the car's four cameras while driving and while parked, with no extra device to buy.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

## BYD's own recording options

- **Driving recorder (some models):** BYD's Atto 3 and Sealion 5 manuals describe a camera at the top centre of the windscreen that faces forward only. Recordings are written to a microSD card in the centre console; for the Atto 3 the manual recommends a 64–128 GB, Class 10, FAT32 card.
- **Accessory camera:** on some models the same kind of front camera is fitted later by the dealer behind the rear-view mirror. Some newly built cars do not have the cable it needs.
- **360° cameras:** they do not record in Turkey and Europe; they only show an image on the screen while parking and manoeuvring. In China, recording with these cameras came to some models by update in 2026.
- **Models without a recorder:** according to the Australian manuals, the Sealion 7, Atto 3 EVO and Atto 2 DM-i have no separate recorder section.

So most BYDs either record nothing at all or record only with the front camera. The rear, left and right sides are not recorded.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do BYD's 360° cameras record?

Not in Turkey and Europe; the 360° cameras only show a live image while parking and manoeuvring. In China, recording with these cameras came to some models by update. Yoldaş records with the same four cameras.

### Does BYD have a factory dashcam?

Some models do: the Atto 3 and Sealion 5 manuals describe a single-camera "driving recorder" facing through the windscreen; recordings are written to a microSD card in the centre console. On some models the same camera is fitted later as an accessory behind the rear-view mirror.

### Do I need to buy a separate dashcam?

No. Yoldaş records while driving and while parked with BYD's own four cameras; no cable, mounting or memory card is needed.

### Where are the recordings saved?

Yoldaş's recordings stay in the car, in the Yoldaş folder, and are not uploaded to the server on their own. You fetch any recording you want from the car on your phone and download it as an MP4.

### Is the recording from a crash deleted?

On hard braking, the moments before and after the event are protected automatically; you can also protect an important recording by hand. Protected recordings are not deleted when the storage space fills up.
