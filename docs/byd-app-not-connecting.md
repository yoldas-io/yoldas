# BYD app not connecting to the car: fixes

Can't add your car in BYD's official app, or do you see "vehicle not responding", "weak network signal" or a service error? This guide lists the right steps to add the car to the app, the common causes and the fixes to try. If you are looking for a phone app that does not depend on BYD's servers, take a look at Yoldaş too.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

## Adding the car to the BYD app the right way

1. **Create the account with the right email**

In the BYD app, create the account with the email address registered at the dealer when you bought the car, and choose the country where you bought it. According to BYD's guide, if a different email or country is chosen, registration fails or the car's data does not appear.
2. **Confirm the verification email**

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or try another address.
3. **Add the car**

Add the car with its chassis number (VIN) or by scanning the QR code under **Settings › System › Remote control** on the car screen. Menu names can differ by model and software.
4. **No QR code? Contact the dealer**

If no QR code appears on the car screen, the car's remote services have not been activated yet. Ask the dealer to activate your account and your car.

## Common errors and what to try

### "Vehicle not responding" or "weak network signal"

- Remote control needs both the phone and the car to be connected to the internet. If the car is in an underground car park or a place with no reception, move it to an open area and try again.
- If the car's location looks out of date in the app, the car's SIM connection may have dropped.
- Switch the car on once and off again, then try again a few minutes later.

### Service error or "vehicle function not configured"

- Update the app; if that doesn't help, delete and reinstall it and sign in again.
- Restart the car screen. According to the BYD manual, on some models holding the scroll button on the steering wheel for 3 seconds restarts the multimedia system.
- There may be a general outage on BYD's servers; the car's own buttons still work in the meantime. Try again after a while.

### The car won't add, or activation is pending

- According to BYD owners, activation can take days, sometimes weeks; BYD does not send a separate "ready" email.
- If another account has linked the car, you cannot add it: a BYD has only one owner at a time, and other people get access through authorisation.

### If none of this worked

Contact your BYD service or the dealer you bought the car from with your chassis number (VIN), your BYD account email and a screenshot of the error.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add my car to the BYD app?

Create your BYD account with the email address registered at the dealer and choose the country where you bought the car. Add the car with its chassis number (VIN) or by scanning the QR code under Settings › System › Remote control on the car screen. If there is no QR code, ask the dealer to activate the car's remote services.

### Why won't the BYD app connect to my car?

The most common reasons: the car's own SIM or data service is offline, the car has not yet been activated by the dealer, the account was created with a different email address or country, or the app is out of date. Remote control needs both the phone and the car to be connected to the internet.

### I get a service error in the BYD app. What should I do?

Update the app or delete and reinstall it, then sign out and sign in again. Restart the car screen and try again after a while. If the problem continues, contact your BYD service with your chassis number, account email and a screenshot of the error.

### Can someone else use the car from the app too?

On a BYD, only one owner links the car at a time; other people are given access through the authorisation section in the app. Unlocking remotely also needs an operation password.

### Is there an app I can use instead of the BYD app?

The Yoldaş phone app is independent of BYD's servers; it talks to Yoldaş in your car. Car status, lock, climate, live camera and Sentry notifications are all in this app.
